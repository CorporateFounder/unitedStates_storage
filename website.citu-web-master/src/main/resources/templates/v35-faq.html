V35 FAQ

Is V35 active?

No. V35 is not active while V35_RESCUE_FORK_INDEX is Long.MAX_VALUE.

Has the founder burn happened?

No. The founder burn has not happened until a burn transaction hash and burn block are published.

What is BURN_ADDRESS?

BURN_ADDRESS is a protocol sink configured for the planned founder burn.

Can BURN_ADDRESS spend?

Spending FROM BURN_ADDRESS is blocked after V35 by consensus policy. Sending TO BURN_ADDRESS is allowed.

What is actual vs modeled supply?

Actual burned supply is based on the BURN_ADDRESS balance. Modeled founder burn is a projection and is not actual burned supply.

How does jackpot choose winners?

The jackpot uses deterministic delayed future block hashes and selects the minerAddress of an eligible mined block.

Can senders win jackpot?

No. Sender addresses are never jackpot participants.

Why 8 GOLD/block?

V35 uses 8 GOLD/block: 6 GOLD miner reward, 2 GOLD jackpot reserve, and 0 founder reward.

Why 432 blocks/day?

V35 uses a 200 sec/block target, which gives 432 blocks/day.

What happens to old blocks?

Old blocks before V35 remain legacy blocks. V35 rules are gated behind the fork index.
