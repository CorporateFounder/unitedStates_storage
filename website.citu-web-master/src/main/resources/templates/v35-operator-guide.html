V35 exchange and node operator guide

No production upgrade is required yet

V35 is prepared but not active yet.

V35 is not active while V35_RESCUE_FORK_INDEX is Long.MAX_VALUE.

Do not manually change V35_RESCUE_FORK_INDEX or V35_BURN_ADDRESS.

Wait for the final notice

The final official notice must include:

  • Exact fork height.
  • Final reviewed tag.
  • Final artifact names.
  • Final SHA-256 checksums.
  • Estimated activation time.
  • Upgrade deadline.
  • Rollback and monitoring instructions.
  • Support/contact channel.

Do not use unofficial artifacts

  • Do not upgrade from draft or dry-run artifacts.
  • Do not announce a final fork height until the official notice is published.
  • Do not create founder burn, fork activation, or jackpot payout transactions manually.
  • Do not send coins to unofficial or fake burn addresses.
