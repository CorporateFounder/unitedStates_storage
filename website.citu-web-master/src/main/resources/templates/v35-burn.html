V35 burn policy and proof

V35 is not active while V35_RESCUE_FORK_INDEX is Long.MAX_VALUE.

The founder burn has not happened until a burn transaction hash and burn block are published.

Modeled founder burn is not actual burned supply.

Burn address policy

  • Official burn address has not been published yet.
  • BURN_ADDRESS is an internal protocol sink name, not a public instruction to send funds.
  • Do not send coins to any unofficial burn address.
  • Founder burn is planned manually before V35 activation.
  • Recommended founder burn: 80%.
