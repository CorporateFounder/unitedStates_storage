  1. Check the published snapshot height and snapshot block hash.

  4. Check that the burn transaction recipient exactly matches the officially published burn address.
  5. Confirm the amount equals 80% of the snapshot founder balance.
  6. Confirm after-burn supply APIs reflect the burn.
  7. Confirm the officially published burn address cannot spend after V35.
