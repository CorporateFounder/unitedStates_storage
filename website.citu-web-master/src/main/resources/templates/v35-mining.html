V35 mining

V35 is not active while V35_RESCUE_FORK_INDEX is Long.MAX_VALUE.

Manual difficulty is legacy-only after V35.

Reward and proof of work

  • One block reward = 6 GOLD instant + jackpot ticket.
  • No founder reward after V35.
  • Bitcoin-like target difficulty: hash <= target.
  • Emergency difficulty easing is part of the V35 safety model.
  • cumulativeWork decides stronger chain after V35.
  • Block time target: 200 sec/block and 432 blocks/day.
